# Weekly review workflow

## Purpose

Refresh current leadership context from recent evidence and choose where attention is most useful next.

## Inputs

- `me/_profile.md`
- the current `me/weekly-digest.md`
- recent relevant people and workstream digests
- supporting raw entries for changed or uncertain claims
- `me/decisions-log.md`
- the review period

## Steps

1. Confirm the review period and planning horizon.
2. Read current digests before raw history.
3. Verify material changes against dated evidence.
4. Compare commitments with observable outcomes.
5. Separate facts, interpretation, confirmed decisions, needed decisions, and open questions.
6. Identify up to three areas for leadership attention.
7. Draft updates using the weekly-leadership-review skill.
8. Review and approve writeback.
9. Update the weekly digest and confirmed decision records.

## Allowed files

- `me/weekly-digest.md`
- `me/decisions-log.md`
- relevant `people/*/digest.md`
- relevant `workstreams/*/digest.md`
- raw logs only for approved correction notes or new evidence

## Approval boundaries

Approval is required for all digest rewrites, final decisions, changes to owners or commitments, consequential people judgments, and deletion or reinterpretation of raw history.

## Failure behavior

- If evidence is missing, add an open question rather than a conclusion.
- If sources conflict, show the conflict and identify the accountable resolver.
- If the review grows into exhaustive reporting, return to the decisions and attention that matter.
- If private material is out of scope, omit it and note the boundary.

## Exit criteria

- the weekly digest has a current date and planning horizon
- material facts have source paths and dates
- interpretations are labeled
- confirmed and needed decisions are distinct
- commitments have owners and checkpoints
- proposed writeback has been approved or remains unapplied
