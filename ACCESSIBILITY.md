# Accessibility

Leadership context should be usable by people with disabilities, including people who use assistive technology. We want everyone to be able to read, adapt, and contribute to this starter without needing a particular app or way of working.

## Scope and current status

This project is a collection of Markdown documents, YAML records, templates, and AI skills, not a standalone application. The core practice remains readable in a plain text editor without Copilot or a plugin. See [README.md](README.md) and [ARCHITECTURE.md](ARCHITECTURE.md) for the project structure and portability principles.

As of 2026-10-22, this statement does not establish accessibility conformance or verified screen reader support. Accessibility also depends on the editor, Markdown renderer, or AI app you choose; this repository cannot guarantee those tools' behavior.

## Contributing accessible content

When adding or changing documentation, templates, or skills:

- Use descriptive headings in a logical hierarchy and links that make sense out of context.
- Keep instructions clear, concise, and available as text, not only in an image, diagram, or recording.
- Add meaningful text alternatives for informative images. Explain diagrams in nearby prose.
- Keep tables simple, with column headers. Prefer a list or prose when a table would be difficult to navigate.
- Do not rely on color, symbols, or visual position alone to communicate meaning.
- Keep the practice usable without a specific app, plugin, or AI model.

For documentation changes, check both the source and rendered Markdown. Check heading navigation, link purpose, and whether the content remains understandable without diagrams. When checking with a keyboard or screen reader, record the tool and environment used and any barriers found; do not treat an automated check alone as proof of accessibility.

See [CONTRIBUTING.md](CONTRIBUTING.md) for the broader contribution guidelines.

## Reporting a barrier

Please [open an issue](https://github.com/alliethu/leadership-os-starter/issues) with "Accessibility" in the title. Include what you can:

- The affected file or section and what you were trying to do.
- What happened and what you expected instead.
- Your editor or browser and, if relevant, assistive technology and version.
- A suggested improvement, if you have one.

You do not need to disclose a disability or diagnosis. Share only a minimal, sanitized example, never private leadership notes, employee information, credentials, or private message transcripts. Follow [PRIVACY.md](PRIVACY.md). If you cannot use GitHub Issues, someone you trust can submit a sanitized report on your behalf.
