# Decisions log

Record only confirmed decisions. Keep proposals in the relevant digest until the accountable person decides.

| Date | Decision | Accountable person | Rationale | Source | Review signal |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| YYYY-MM-DD | Add a confirmed decision | Add role or self | Add concise rationale | Add path and date | What outcome should trigger review? |

## Entry guidance

- **Date:** when the decision became final
- **Decision:** the choice, not the discussion
- **Accountable person:** who owns the decision
- **Rationale:** the evidence and tradeoff that mattered
- **Source:** a relative path and date
- **Review signal:** evidence that should prompt confirmation, adjustment, or reversal

Use `templates/decision.md` when a decision needs more context than one row.
